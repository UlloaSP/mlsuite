## Navigation

The navigation has up to three groups, always in this order:

| Group | Entries | Shown to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Explore | Everyone, with or without an account. |
| Workspace | Predict, Models, Schemas, Inferences, API, Plugins, Review | Members, each entry only with the permission it needs. |
| Administration | Organizations, Users, Moderation, API traffic, Infra | Superadmins. |

A group with nothing you may open is left out. Each section remembers where you were in it on this
device, so its entry takes you back there when you come from another section.

The navigation is a right-hand sidebar by default. In
[Settings › Layout](/account/settings/#layout) you can move it to the left, top or bottom, make it
float, or start it collapsed. **Collapse sidebar** (`Ctrl`/`⌘` + `B`) shrinks it to icons.

Below the groups, **Tooling** holds **User guide** and **Global search**, and the account menu at
the bottom opens Profile, Notifications, Settings and **Sign out**.

## The organization switcher

Everything in the Workspace group belongs to the organization shown at the top of the navigation.
Its menu opens that organization's Overview, Members, Invitations, Roles & templates and Settings
(each one when your permissions allow it) and lists your other organizations under **Switch
organization**. Choosing one switches to it and opens its home page. See
[Organizations](/organizations/overview/).

## Global search

Press `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `K`, or choose **Global search**, and type at least two characters. Results
are grouped as organizations, models, schemas, snapshots, bookmarks, inferences and plugins, five
per group, all from the current organization except your organizations. Use the arrow keys and
`Enter` to open one, `Esc` to close.

Search needs the **View plugins** permission. A member without it, such as someone whose only role
is Reviewer, sees "Search is unavailable".

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Alt` + `1`…`9` | Open the Nth navigation entry; Explore is always `Alt` + `1`. Hold `Alt` to see the numbers. |
| `Alt` + `Shift` + `1`…`9` | Open the Nth page of the current section, such as a schema's tabs. |
| `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `K` | Global search. |
| `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `B` | Collapse or expand the sidebar. |
| `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `Shift` + `L` | Cycle the color scheme: System, Light, Dark. |
| `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `Shift` + `F` | Enter or leave fullscreen. |

The last four can be changed in [Settings › Keybindings](/account/settings/#keybindings). Number
shortcuts do nothing while you type in a field.

## The tour

**User guide** starts a short tour of the navigation: each step points at one entry and says what
it is for. Use **Next** and **Previous**, or click outside the tour to close it. Replay it whenever
you like.
