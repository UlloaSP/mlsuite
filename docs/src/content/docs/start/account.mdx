## Register

1. Open the instance, for example [mlsuite.irlab.org](https://mlsuite.irlab.org/), and choose
   **Create account** in the header. The form is also at `/login?mode=register`.
2. Enter your **Full name**, **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Create account**.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Full name | Up to 150 characters. |
| Email | A valid address, not already registered. It is stored in lowercase. |
| Password | 10 to 128 characters. |

There is no email verification and no separate username: MLsuite derives the username from the
part of the email before `@`. If registration fails, the form only says "We couldn't create your
account"; an address that is already registered is the most common cause.

A new account gets a **personal organization** named "&lt;Full name&gt; Personal", in which it is
the Owner. You can upload models and run them there straight away.

## Sign in and out

Sign in at `/login` with your email and password. After signing in, or registering, MLsuite returns
you to the page you came from; with no page to return to, it opens Explore. Opening a workspace
page without a session sends you to the sign-in page and back again afterwards.

Sign out from the account menu at the bottom of the navigation. If the current session holds runs
you have not saved, MLsuite asks first: **Discard and sign out** loses them.

The session is a browser cookie. Neither the app nor the API offers a password reset: on a
self-hosted instance, a [superadmin](/admin/users/) can set a new password for you.

## Change your details

The **Profile** page shows your name, email, system role, current organization and your role in
it. It is read-only: name, email and password changes are made by a superadmin.
