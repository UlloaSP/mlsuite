## Explore

<Capture
  name="explore"
  alt="The Explore feed seen without an account, searched for 'loan', showing one public bookmark card."
/>

**Explore** lists every bookmark published on the instance. It is the first navigation entry for
members and the home page for visitors without an account, whose header offers **Sign in** and
**Create account**; both bring you back to the page you were on.

A person who publishes with a public [profile](/account/settings/#your-profile) is linked from each
of their bookmarks, and their profile lists all they publish.

Search by bookmark name, description or publisher, and sort by **Latest updated** or **Name**. Each
card shows the bookmark's name and description, how many inputs it asks for and reports it gives
back, the publishing organization and when it was updated.

## A public page

<Capture
  name="public-bookmark"
  alt="A public bookmark page: name, description, publisher, a note from the organization, an example picker, the form and the Your runs panel."
/>

The page shows the bookmark's name and description, its publisher, input and report counts, and
the publisher's note, with its links and DOIs made clickable. Members of the publishing
organization also see **Open in workspace**.

To run it:

1. Optionally choose a record under **Start from an example**. It replaces what is in the form, and
   every value can be edited afterwards.
2. Fill the form and choose **Run**. The results appear in the **Results** tab.

A line under the form says how many runs you have left. Each caller may run one bookmark a limited
number of times in 24 hours, counted from their first run of it:

| Caller | Runs per bookmark per 24 hours (default) |
| --- | --- |
| Without an account, per network address | 50 |
| Signed in, per account | 500 |

Past the limit, the form asks you to sign in or says when you can run again. When too many public
runs are in progress, a run is refused with "The models are busy"; try again a moment later. Runs
the server could not perform are not counted.

## Your runs

Every run you make is kept, with its results, under **Your runs**, newest first, for this browser.
MLsuite recognizes the browser by a cookie set on the first run and kept for a year; clearing it
loses the list, though not the runs themselves, which the publishing organization keeps. Open a run
to see its results and the inputs it ran with; **Back to form** returns to the form. A run made on
an earlier version of the form shows only its inputs.

## Review a run

<Capture
  name="public-run-review"
  alt="A run opened from Your runs: its Decision report and, below it, the Review this run form at its first step."
/>

Under an opened run, **Review this run** shows the bookmark's review steps: an assessment of each
prediction and the questions of its [questionnaires](/feedback/questionnaires/). Choose **Save
review**; **Edit** changes it later. The run is marked **Reviewed** in Your runs once every step is
answered. No account is needed. Your answers reach the publisher as feedback from "Visitor", or
from your account when you are signed in.

## Link previews

Links to a public page unfurl in chat apps and social networks with the bookmark's name, its
description (or its note) and the organization's logo, or the MLsuite mark when it has none.
