Everything you do in MLsuite belongs to an account. Its models, schemas, bookmarks, inferences,
reviews, plugins and API clients are visible only to the people in it.

- **Personal account.** Every user has one from the moment they sign up, under their username. It
  is theirs alone: it has no members, invitations, roles or settings, takes its name from their
  profile, and lasts as long as they do.
- **Organizations.** Anyone can create one from **New organization** in the switcher and becomes its
  Owner; a [superadmin](/admin/users/) may choose another Owner. Members join by
  [invitation](/organizations/members/), and each has a [role](/organizations/roles/).

Usernames and organization handles share one namespace, as on GitHub: a handle has 1 to 39 letters,
digits or single hyphens, is stored in lowercase, cannot start or end with a hyphen, and cannot be
one that is already taken or that collides with a page of the app, such as `admin` or `settings`.

Switch from the switcher at the top of the navigation: your personal account comes first, then your
organizations. The switch applies to that browser tab: another tab stays where it is, so two tabs
can work in two accounts side by side. Your latest choice is remembered and a new tab opens there;
accepting an invitation switches you to the organization that invited you. Creating an organization
leaves you where you are and offers to switch to it. If you are removed from the organization you
work in, or it is deleted, your next page opens in your personal account.

## Overview

<Capture
  name="organization-overview"
  alt="An organization overview: model lifecycle counts for models, schemas, inferences and reviews, newest members, latest invitations and plugins."
/>

The overview opens from the switcher, or as the home page of members with **View workspace**. In an
organization it shows your role badge, a **Model lifecycle** row counting Models, Schemas, Inferences and Reviews
(each with a link and a hint when empty), the newest members, the latest invitations and the
plugin count.

## Settings

Open **Settings** from the switcher or the overview. Changing anything here needs **Edit
organization**; the page itself needs **View organization**.

<Capture
  name="organization-settings"
  alt="Organization settings: Identity with name, slug and description, and Logo with a drop zone."
/>

**Identity**
: Name (up to 150 characters), handle (up to 39, unique among users and organizations) and description (up to
  600). Choose **Save changes**.

**Logo**
: Drop a PNG or JPG of at least 64 × 64 pixels and up to 2 MB. MLsuite keeps the centre square and
  stores it as a 256-pixel PNG, so a square picture is shown whole. The logo appears beside the
  organization's name in the workspace, on its [public pages](/publishing/public-pages/) and in
  link previews of them. **Remove logo** deletes it.

**Ownership**
: An organization has one Owner. **Transfer ownership** (needs **Transfer ownership**) makes
  another active member the Owner; the previous Owner becomes Admin.

**Danger zone**
: **Delete organization** (needs **Delete organization**) removes it permanently, but only an empty
  organization can be deleted: one without models, schemas, plugins, reviews, invitations or audit
  events. Any invitation ever sent, in any state, keeps it from being deleted.

There is no way to leave an organization yourself; a member with **Remove members** removes you.
