The account menu at the bottom of the navigation opens **Profile**, **Notifications**,
**Settings** and **Sign out**.

- **Profile** shows your name, email, system role, current organization and role, and this
  browser's language, time zone and theme. It is read-only.
- **Notifications** tells you what happened while you were elsewhere: bulk uploads that finished
  or were stopped, models that became ready or could not be prepared, invitations answered, and
  reviews assigned to you. Each one leads to what it is about and switches to its organization when
  needed. Invitations waiting for you come first, with **Accept** and **Decline**. The account menu
  counts what you have not read, and a notification you have read is removed after 30 days.

## Settings

  name="settings"
  alt="Settings, Appearance tab: System, Light and Dark color schemes and the built-in theme cards."
/>

Settings apply at once and are saved in this browser only; another browser starts from the
defaults.

### Appearance

- **Color scheme**: System (default), Light or Dark.
- **Themes**: eshkra (default), MLsuite, Airbnb, Grove, Ocean, Ember, Iris, Graphite, Nord,
  Catppuccin, Rosé Pine, Tokyo Night, Gruvbox and Solarized. Click a card to use the theme in both schemes, or
  one of its two orbs to use it only in that scheme.
- **Create theme**: name a theme (up to 40 characters) and give its light and dark variants six
  colors each: background, surface, muted surface, text, muted text and accent. Text must keep a
  4.5:1 contrast with the background and surfaces, and the accent 3:1.
- **Contrast**: raise contrast from 100% to 125%.

### Typography

- **Interface font**: Manrope (default), Inter, Geist, IBM Plex Sans, Source Sans 3, Figtree, DM
  Sans, Atkinson Hyperlegible or the system font, at 14 to 18 px (16 by default).
- **Monospace font**: DM Mono (default), JetBrains Mono, Geist Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Fira Code,
  Source Code Pro or the system font, at 12 to 16 px (13 by default).
- **Word wrap** in code editors and previews, on by default.

### Keybindings

Change the shortcuts for global search, the color scheme, fullscreen and the sidebar. Click one,
press the new combination, or `Esc` to cancel; **Reset all** restores the
[defaults](/start/navigation/#keyboard-shortcuts). A shortcut needs `Ctrl`, `⌘` or `Alt` with one
key. `Alt` with a number is reserved for navigation, and common browser shortcuts such as `Ctrl` +
`T` are refused.

### Layout

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Navigation position | Left, Right (default), Top, Bottom |
| Navigation style | Fixed (default), Floating |
| Location of the breadcrumb | Above the title (default), at the bottom, as a rail on the left or right, or off |
| Collapsed navigation | Start with the sidebar collapsed |
| Fullscreen | Enter fullscreen, when the browser allows it |
