## The review tray

The tray on the right sorts your assigned inferences into two groups:

- **Pending**: nothing saved yet.
- **Revision**: answers saved and ready to send.

The first item opens on its own; choose any item to open it. Inferences of expired reviews leave
the inbox.

## Answer an inference

The main panel shows the inference's name, the review form and two collapsible sections,
**Outputs** (the reports as the run drew them) and **Inputs**. The form is a sequence of steps,
one or two per report:

1. **Assessment**, for classifier and regressor reports: choose the class you consider right, or
   type the value you consider right. Your answer is what the export calls the reviewer's feedback
   on the prediction.
2. **&lt;Report&gt; review**, when the report has a [questionnaire](/feedback/questionnaires/): its
   questions, such as a rating, a choice or a comment.

Questions are required unless the schema says otherwise. Move with **Next** and **Previous**, then
choose **Save review**. Your answers are shown read-only, with **Edit** to change them, and the
inference moves to **Revision**.

When several models answer the same report, one answer applies to all of them.

If the snapshot asks nothing, the panel says so and offers **Complete this review**, which submits
this inference alone.

## Complete your reviews

**Complete review (N)** at the top of the tray submits every inference in Revision at once, across
all your reviews. Submitted inferences leave the inbox and can no longer be changed by you; a
member who manages reviews can [reopen](/feedback/reviews/#reopen-or-delete-a-response) one for
you.
