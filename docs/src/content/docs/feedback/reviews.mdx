---
title: Reviews
description: Assign inferences to reviewers, follow their progress, read their answers, reopen a review or delete a response.
---

A review asks organization members to give feedback on a set of inferences of one schema snapshot
before a date. Each reviewer answers each inference: whether the prediction was right, and the
review questions of its reports. Their answers appear in the Inferences table and in the
[CSV export](/predictions/export/).

Creating and following reviews needs **Manage reviews**. Reviewers need **Review**; the built-in
**Reviewer** role has exactly that.

## Create a review

<Capture
  name="review-create"
  alt="The Create review dialog: a schema snapshot and bookmark, a list of selected inferences, a reviewer list and a Review expires date."
/>

1. On **Inferences**, filter the table to the runs you want reviewed, then choose **Create
   review**. The dialog starts from the whole filtered result.
2. Pick the **Schema snapshot** when the result spans several; a review covers one snapshot. A
   **Bookmark** can narrow it further.
3. Select the **Inferences**. All start selected.
4. Select the **Reviewers**: active members whose role includes **Review**. If none are listed,
   give someone that permission first.
5. Set **Review expires**. It defaults to 30 days from today and ends at 23:59 UTC that day.
6. Choose **Assign review**.

The inferences appear in each reviewer's [Review inbox](/feedback/answer/). MLsuite sends no email
or notification; tell the reviewers. The review questions come from the snapshot itself, see
[Feedback questionnaires](/feedback/questionnaires/). Reviewers and inferences cannot be changed
after the review is created, and a review cannot be extended: create another one.

## Follow a review

<Capture
  name="inference-reviews"
  alt="The Reviews tab of an inference with one completed assignment showing who requested it and when it was submitted."
/>

Open an inference and its **Reviews** tab, or choose **Review status** in its row menu. Each tile is
one reviewer's assignment, with a state:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Nothing saved yet. |
| In progress | Answers saved, not yet submitted. |
| Completed | Submitted. |

Tiles also show who requested the review, when it was submitted and when it expires, with an
**Expired** badge once the date has passed. Filter by state, search by reviewer or requester, and
sort by latest requested, latest submitted or reviewer. The tab refreshes while it is open.
Open a tile to read that reviewer's answers.

The Feedback column of the table says whether an inference's questions are answered:
**Completed** once every step has saved answers that agree, **Pending** otherwise, and **Not
configured** when its snapshot asks nothing.

## Reopen or delete a response

A tile's menu offers:

| Action | When | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reopen | Completed, and the review has not expired. | The inference returns to the reviewer's inbox as In progress, with their answers kept. |
| Delete response | In progress or Completed. | Deletes the reviewer's answers for this inference in this review. Their answers in other reviews of the inference, and on a public page, stay. The assignment stays and returns to Pending. |

A reviewer's answers belong to the review they were given in: an inference in two reviews gets
an answer in each. Every answer given, changed or deleted is kept in the result's answer history
(`GET /api/prediction-result-feedback/history?resultId=…`), with who gave it, what it said and
who changed it when, so a label stays auditable after it changes or goes.

An inference that belongs to a review cannot be deleted.
